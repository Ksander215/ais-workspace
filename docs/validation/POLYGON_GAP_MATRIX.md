## Measurement discipline

Do not score an answer by subjective impression alone. For material claims, record the repository evidence used to verify or falsify the claim. A single question may produce multiple findings.

Do not aggregate scores into a single percentage until the verification method and sampling rules are defined. Qualitative findings and evidence quality take precedence over a cosmetic benchmark score.
